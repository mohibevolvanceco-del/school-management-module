Project: Plan a School Event

Your task

Choose one school event, such as Sports Day, a Science Fair, a Parent-Teacher Meeting or a Prize Distribution, and write a management plan for it as if you were the person in charge.

1. Goal

What the event should achieve, written so it can be checked.

2. Team and roles

A table showing who is responsible for what.

3. Timeline

Key dates from four weeks before to the day after.

4. Budget

A simple spreadsheet of costs and total.

5. Communication plan

Who you will tell what, when, and through which channel.

6. Risks and backups

Three things that could go wrong and your backup plan.

Finish with a 150 to 200 word reflection on which digital tools you used and how they helped.

Tools you can use

  • Google Calendar for the timeline
  • Google Sheets for the team table and budget
  • Google Forms if you want a sample sign-up or feedback form
  • Paper is fine too. Take clear photos of each page.

How to submit

  1. Put all six parts and your reflection into one PDF.
  2. Name it RollNo_Name_Event_Plan.pdf.
  3. Upload it through the submission form shared by your teacher.
  4. Deadline: Friday of Week 2, 4:00 p.m.

Marking guide (40 marks)

CriterionStrong workWeak workMarks
Goal and timelineClear, checkable goal and realistic datesVague goal, missing dates8
Team and budgetEvery task has an owner, budget adds upRoles unclear, costs missing8
Communication planRight channel for each audienceOne channel for everything8
Risks and backupsRealistic risks with practical backupsRisks listed with no plan8
