Project

Design a mini School Management System

You will design the database for a small SMS that a school could actually use. You do not need to write code. The focus is on planning the data properly.

Your design must include

  • At least four tables (for example Student, Class, Attendance and Fee or Result).
  • The fields in each table, with the primary key marked.
  • At least two foreign keys linking the tables.
  • Three sample records in each table, using made-up data only.
  • A short access table showing what an admin, a teacher and a parent can see.
  • A 150 to 200 word explanation of your design choices.

Tools you can use

  • draw.io for the table diagram (free, no account needed)
  • Google Sheets or Excel for the sample records, one sheet per table
  • Paper is fine too. Take clear photos of each page.

How to submit

  1. Put your diagram, sample records and explanation into one PDF.
  2. Name it RollNo_Name_SMS_Project.pdf.
  3. Upload it through the submission form shared by your teacher.
  4. Deadline: Friday of Week 2, 4:00 p.m.
