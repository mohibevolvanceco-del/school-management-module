Group Activity

Case study: Green Valley School

Green Valley School has 600 students. Attendance is kept in paper registers, fees are recorded in a notebook in the accounts office, and results are typed into separate Excel files by each teacher. Last month, the school sent fee reminders to three families who had already paid, and the principal could not quickly find which students had missed more than ten days.

Your task (groups of three)

  1. List three problems Green Valley School has because of its current system.
  2. Choose the two SMS modules the school should start with, and explain why.
  3. Suggest one rule to protect student data when the new system is introduced.

How to take part

  • By Wednesday: one member posts your group's answer in the class WhatsApp group.
  • By Friday: each member replies to one other group's post with a question or suggestion.
  • Keep it respectful, and do not share any real student's personal details.
