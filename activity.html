Group Activity

Case study: A Difficult Monday

It’s Monday morning at Green Valley School. Within the first hour, the principal faces three problems:

  1. Two teachers are absent, and nobody is covering their classes.
  2. Several parents are angry because last week’s test results still haven’t been shared.
  3. The electricity is out, and the computer lab exam starts at 11:00 a.m.

Your task (groups of three)

Imagine you’re the school’s management team. For each problem, decide:

  • what you’d do first, and who you’d ask to help
  • how you’d communicate it, and through which channel
  • one thing the school could set up in advance so it’s easier next time

How to take part

  • By Wednesday: one member posts your group’s plan in the class WhatsApp group.
  • By Friday: each member replies to another group with one question or suggestion.
  • Be respectful, and don’t name real teachers or students.
