Welcome

Behind every well-run school is a good manager: someone who plans the year, organises people and resources, leads the staff and keeps checking what can be improved. In this module you will learn what school managers actually do, what makes them good at it, and how simple digital tools can help them do the job better.

Who this module is for

Grade 11 students, especially those in leadership roles such as prefects, house captains and student council members, or anyone interested in leading a school one day.

Length: two weeks

What you will be able to do

  1. Describe what a school manager does and what makes a good one.
  2. Plan and organise a school activity using simple digital tools.
  3. Choose the right way to communicate with staff, students and parents.
  4. Use school data to make fair decisions while keeping it private.
