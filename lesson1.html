Lesson 1: What is an SMS?

From registers to software

Think about how your school keeps records on paper. Attendance is in one register, fees in another, and results in a file in the exam office. If the principal wants to know which students have low attendance and unpaid fees, someone has to search through several registers by hand.

A School Management System solves this by storing all records in one shared database. Staff enter data once, and anyone with permission can search, update or print reports in seconds.

Paper registers

  • Slow to search
  • Same data written in many places
  • Easy to lose or damage
  • Reports made by hand

School Management System

  • Search in seconds
  • Each fact stored once
  • Backed up automatically
  • Reports generated instantly

The main modules

An SMS is split into parts called modules. Each module handles one area of school work:

ModuleWhat it storesWho uses it most
AdmissionsNew students' details and documentsOffice staff
AttendanceDaily present / absent / late recordsClass teachers
ExaminationsMarks, grades and report cardsTeachers, exam office
FeesFee amounts, payments and receiptsAccounts office
TimetableWhich class has which subject, whenCoordinators
CommunicationNotices and messages to parentsAdmin, teachers
