Course Plan

The module runs over two weeks: six classes plus about one hour of work at home each week.

WeekClassesWhat you will doDue
Week 1Lessons 1 and 2Learn what a school manager does, then practise planning with digital toolsSelf-check quiz by Saturday
Week 2Lesson 3, activity, projectLearn about leading people and using data, solve the group case study, and plan your own school eventGroup post by Wednesday, project by Friday

How you are assessed

Quiz: 0%

Practice only. Use it to check your understanding.

Group activity: 25%

Solve a real school problem as a team.

Project: 75%

Plan a complete school event.

Tip: work through the pages in order. The project uses ideas from all three lessons.
